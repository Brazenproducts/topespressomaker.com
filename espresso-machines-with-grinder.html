What should a beginner look for in an espresso machine?

Start with a machine that has a pressurized portafilter basket (more forgiving of inconsistent grinds), a built-in steam wand, and PID temperature control. The Breville Barista Express checks all three and adds a built-in grinder — the most complete beginner-to-intermediate package.

How long does it take to pull a shot of espresso?

Extraction takes 25-30 seconds from the moment you start the pump. Add 20-30 seconds for pre-heating the portafilter, 10 seconds for tamping, and 30-60 seconds for steaming milk. Total time: about 3-4 minutes for a latte from a cold start. Most machines have a fast heat-up time of 15-30 seconds.
